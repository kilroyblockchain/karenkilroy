Running experiments from here

Not yet wired up. Submitting circuits from this page spends real IBM Quantum quota, so the runner is being built behind a proper identity check rather than a forgeable header — a public endpoint that can spend your quota needs to verify who is calling it, not just trust what the caller claims.

Until it lands, runs go through claude.ai/code against the kilroyblockchain/quantum repository, or from your own machine with qiskit/bell-hardware.py --real.

Ledger integrity is checked two ways, on purpose. Chain linkage is verified live in your browser. Content hashes are verified at publish time by provenance/ledger.py — the same code that computed them — because a verifier that disagrees with the writer is worse than none.
